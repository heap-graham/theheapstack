“A Japanese Lady of Serenity - A Spark of Hope and Tranquility”

Part 1 — Our First Meeting


The first time I met you,
you were just outside my home, speaking on your phone;
we looked at each other, both standing there alone …

We greeted each other,
and you were so polite, so respectful,
with traditional bows: a Japanese lady of subtle beauty and simple elegance,
radiating serenity.

I invited you inside and felt a peaceful soul enter my world.

Then, standing together in my kitchen, talking quietly,
I noticed your eyes…
or perhaps, within my daydream, we noticed each other’s eyes.
With only a single glance,
a natural longing to be near you emerged within me …
to hold you,
to kiss you.

And whether your pupils are the deepest black,
or your eyes shine with the purest light,
I cannot truly say…

Because if I dare to look too deeply, and for too long,
I will blush with excitement … especially if you lovingly,
purposefully gaze back at me and smile unabashed.

And I fear that if you notice too much of my attention,
you may run away from me forever.


So my recurring daydream remains this: that the Japanese Lady of Serenity will stay,
and that this spark of hope and tranquility may gently grow,
into a real and lasting friendship,
filled with love, joy, peace and happiness.


なるようになる
(Naru yō ni naru … What will be, will be.)
