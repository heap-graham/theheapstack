"Part 5 - Your "Black Hole" Thinking is Heart-Sinking!"


Black Holes are formed by giant dying stars,
burning so bright;
as their sparkling sky-light fails,
breaking under their own might.

Creating a singularity warping nearby time and space.
With density so great they suck,
drain away,
all energy,
matter in the vicinity.

The event horizon defines the point of no return.
Not even light escapes this dark eternal pit!

Knowing you for four months: April, May, June, July
- A third of a year drifted away,
vanishing in the blink of an eye.

In April, May you were at my side,
wanting, seeking my company.
Spending quality time with our fancy, wild and free.
Suddenly, you switched, stopped talking, a total mystery.
Not making any more of those fun-time, exciting plans with me.

All smiles with respect: full of eastern promise at the start:
then to exhibit modern western female habits which tore my heart apart,
taking my unrequited love for granted.

Tell me, is ghosting me that smart?

My experience of you is unstable,
showing your indifference in your inactions and lack of words.
Becoming oh-so disappointing.
You are not the spontaneous,
loving, caring person your false mask of pretension that you presented.

Now I am wondering who you are?

Cause I cannot see you now,
I do not have a clue who are pretending to be!

For the whole month of June - not having the time of day to give.
That be 30 days or 720 hours or 43,200 minutes of time sucked into your "Black Hole".
Not that I am counting or reckoning for your soul!
Excuses you made,
blaming your boring,
tedious job.
The demands of having to speak English to the daily mob.
Your English declines week by week,
not improving while employed on your humdrum track.

Did I say ?
Right, your busy dead-end job!
Full of challenges for your stunning intellect!

You spend your free time and energy out.
Chasing the darkness into the night, until morning brings the light.

With whom or why,
you simply do not say,
claiming no relationship will get in your way.

Surprised I'd be if you always land on a friend's couch,
on your daily binge to stay up late,
and for what ultimate fate?

Still, it is not my business
and now I am doing my best not to care.
There is no more I have to share.
Our time has finally come to a pointless end.

No more of my time,
my energy will I spend Love-Blind on thee.
My goodwill is gone.
Is measuring way below zero.

Now I have no choice,
for my own sanity,
to freeze you out.

Playing a person's heart because you can,
taking, taking - giving little back,
leads down the shallow road to nowhere.

Sadly,
your western adventure will teach you nothing,
except how to be a "Party-Girl".

Chasing the next juice of experience
and leaving your waking days exhausted,
your life force spent.
Still not realising that in this moment love is for you is always present
- That be God's True-Love, not mine is buried!

There is no excuse,
not even the heart-break from your distant past.
Shutting down your feelings because you were betrayed,
is the wrong path.

Time to stop!
Ponder on what your real values are.

I wish that I could stop caring
and will be sad to see the back of you.

Time for meaningful action is passing quickly.
Your "Black Hole" Thinking is Heart-Sinking.
Sitting in silence and not talking
- is this the way to share your life ?

With my hand on my broken heart
- again I say: "I do not want you to be my loving wife!"

Please, I beg you, open your heart!
