“Quality time spent with you … Anata to sugosu taisetsu na jikan …”

Part 2 - Getting to know each other, step by step

Dai ni bu: - Otagai wo shitte iku koto, ippo ippo


You moved in mid-April, you told me you were alone - only to stay 'till May and I said: "Ok!".

When you wanted to stay 'till next April's showers:
I said: "Fine"; if we keep our respect and we drink lots of wine.

Our first 'date' away from home: Irish Film Institute (IFI),
again alone;
we rushed our feet to the Teachers Club.
We danced our Salsa class; after you had many offers of social dances,
I did not take a chance by going green with envy; nor did I want another man to hold you tight;
not that I had that right.

Next to "The Grand Social", for Guinness or two.
We sat, talking through hours.
Listened to an epic list of western pop:
"Changes", Bowie; "It's a sin", The Pet Shop Boys; "Dani California", the Red Hot Chilli Peppers.
We laughed as they mimicked Prince, the Beatles too, silly talented and caring entertainers.

Time flew by: our moments precious, rare.
Back to the IFI, our conversation took a deeper, personal turn.

Pleasantly surprised you had a Project Manager role,
showing me the true depth of your soul.
You spoke of your calling to be a social worker,
and my growing admiration of you was born.

Later you smiled, teased with a playful squeeze,
mocking my voice: "Just one more Guinness, please!"

You wanted to know how angry I could be,
I was touched you wished to please me - flattered and already,
falling fast,
hiding a love that I hoped would last.
I held myself back, though the longing was true;
I denied my own hands when they wanted to touch you.
Fighting the urge with all of my might,
craving to kiss you into the night.

You shared your dream of me: "As a famous star".
Laughing: I told you of my puzzle plan: "I'd be 'King Jigsaw Man'".
I tried to share the dream I had of you;
you refused, looking away, bright red,
with your fantasy idea of what I may have done with you.

In my dream, you walked in my way, gave me a note, saying:
"This is the Japanese for I Love you".

We spend quality time side by side:
Watching "The Matrix" - 'There is no spoon'.
Then you found "The Secretary"',
a naughty one,
hid at the bottom of my long playlist.

We talked and talked; played and fought, with karate fights: I was outmatched,
as you showed real martial art skills in our time and space.

The memories which stand out:
you beating me at scrabble,
and that last Sunday in May,
running towards me to go salsa dancing;
then after, you fall asleep on the bus home,
resting your head on the shoulder of the kind old lady next to you.

I was wishing all the way back, on that bus journey, that you had fallen asleep on my shoulder …


My treasured memory of you,
sitting with my presents for you - the Salsa outfit and the Owl and Pussycat card.

Two unlikely characters that run away and spend their lives together.

As the silly and romantic children's verse would have you believe;
that a fantasy like that could really happen in reality …
