WIMS - Service Development Directory
====================================

This directory contains all material relating to the development,
implementation and operation of the Weight Insight Mentoring Service (WIMS).

The directory is organised by functional workstreams rather than by software
components.

0.0 WIMS - The Voice
    Identity, philosophy, tone of voice and writing standards.

0.1 WIMS - Service Documentation
    Service definition, specifications, standards and architecture.

0.2 WIMS - Plan
    Roadmaps, releases, implementation plans and project management.

1.0 WIMS Book
    Development of the WIMS book and supporting material.

2.0 Educational Platform
    Website, software, tools and technical platform.

3.0 Educational Content
    Educational articles, lessons, guides and learning resources.

4.0 WIMS Emerging Practical Theory - One Case Study
    The practical development of WIMS through a single long-term case study.

5.0 WIMS Community Engagement
    Community activities, mentoring, workshops and user engagement.

6.0 WIMS Communications
    Newsletters, announcements, promotional material and communications.

ZZZ - Archive
    Historical material retained for reference.
