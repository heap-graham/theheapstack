WIM Support 2026 Spreadsheet User Guide

[1.0] Introduction

The structure of this Google Sheet consists of the following worksheets:

- Your Details
- Target-lbs
- Target-st
- Actuals
- January to December

The monthly worksheets are used to record your actual rested weight.

For the purposes of this workbook, rested weight is defined as the weight measured in the morning after waking and before breakfast.

All other calculations within the workbook are generated automatically.

[1.1] Your Details

Enter your current weight in Stones and Pounds.

The spreadsheet uses this information to generate your target weights throughout the workbook.

[1.2] Target-lbs

Displays your target weight progression in Pounds.

No data entry is required.

[1.3] Target-st

Displays your target weight progression in Stones.

No data entry is required.

[1.4] Actuals

Provides an automatically generated summary of the weight data entered throughout the year.

No data entry is required.

[1.5] January to December

These worksheets are used to record your actual rested weight.

January is shown as the example worksheet. The remaining monthly worksheets operate in exactly the same way.

Enter your weight at approximately the same time each day.

The workbook automatically calculates:

- Weekly Minimum Weight

- Weekly Maximum Weight

- Weekly Average Weight

- Weekly Range

- Weekly Weight Change

- Target Weight

- Net Achievement

**** Important Note ****

Weight naturally fluctuates from day to day. Individual measurements are often less important than weekly and monthly trends.

The workbook provides a structured way to record information and review outcomes over time.
