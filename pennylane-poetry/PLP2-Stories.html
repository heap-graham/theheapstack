Welcome

Welcome to Pennylane Poetry and Short Adult Stories.

This website is a collection of romantic poetry and short adult stories celebrating love, desire, intimacy, humour and the many emotions that connect people throughout life.

Each poem and story reflects a moment, a memory, a dream or an experience, written to entertain, inspire and sometimes simply to make the reader smile.

Pennylane, our much-loved Border Collie, became the inspiration for the name of this collection and remains its gentle mascot.

Thank you for visiting and enjoy your stay.
