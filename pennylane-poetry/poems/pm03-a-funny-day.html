"A Funny Day"
"(And the Cat sat on the Mat in a Mystical way)"


We met alone one sunny day,
in the green park and began to play,
Movies were our starting discourse,
but we had a strained and taut social intercourse,

Being nervous and not myself,
I hid within my deepest private shell.
You were strong and wore bright red,
which made you just so slightly inconspicuous,
(and not so promiscuous)

All the time I made the effort to understand the Lady’s method,
Alas ‘twas untrue that all you wanted was too screw,
my heart,
And maybe what you needed was to take a pew by healing me,
and thus forgiving you.

What’s lost on me and I think you too,
is one day soon we’ll both be gone,
And we’ll never know another good day in the sun
(together).

Perhaps it’s right to walk away,
to live and fight for another day
(apart),
And who knows what’s in store for us,
as we both return to dust.


So what I fancy next is to be a mat and
I’ll hope and pray that you’ll will be my cat!
