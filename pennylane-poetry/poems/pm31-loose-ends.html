“Loose Ends”


The sea breeze sweeps across the yellow sandy beach,
moving gracefully with God's turbulent spiritual tiny hands,
and with sacred knowledge hidden deep within,
while tracing a windy,
coiled path through rich verdant greenery,
falling softly,
whispering a millennium of secrets,
held silently in its sound waves
and folds gently onto my face leaving me colder,
older and slightly bolder,
and still I weep tenderly,
quietly for you
and sleep is my only retreat from all of this
and now the only way to experience your dearly needed bliss,
while I imagine,
easily,
being entwined with your long deep wet,
passionate kisses,
and lost forever and ever,
in your silky, white, velvet caring skin.

As I write these words unspoken
and yearn to be with you,
from now a far distant foreign land,
and my greatest hopes and dreams to be with you,
are dashed,
by you,
once again,
thrashed and crashed on the rugged coastline of my minds romantic landscape,
while I ponder on our recent moments
and a few things begin to emerge which compel me,
with rhyming words to clarify
and convey with a simple parley.

Always I try to write in effortless verse,
and without compunction,
my desire,
almost uncontrollable urge
and my motivations are always the same,
to say that: “I love you”,
without conditions and no reservations,
as said repeatedly,
lovingly,
longingly,
rhyme after rhyme,
verse after verse,
will these prose meet you
and greet you with a treat of true love,
long overdue for your respect
and of course aching to be deep inside of you,

As I recall,
recently,
while moving gently my right hand through your soft, blond, wise hair,
and left hand stroking,
sensitively your warm tummy flat,
in decreasing soothing,
smooth circles,
listening to you recant your lives short stories,
of birth and your worthy notions of children
and your hypnotic occupation,
which rise you above my unworthy,
tortured heart: seeking openly to bond with your sweet and gentle soul.

And as the golden,
honeyed moment fades to the next
and the only action left is to take you quick with succulent,
wet kisses,
with polished fingertip caresses,
to worship you,
inch by inch,
with delight and relish,
and these are the loose ends which come to my mind,
as “It is So Easy” to fall for you,
to please and tease you,
in short to love you.

And wanting to tumble into the deepest parts of you,
to excite and delight you,
do I hold back realising that: as easy as it is to love
and cherish you;
that sooner or later you will be gone from our warm embraces
and whatever treasures we have shared,
we will,
can not complete each other,
maybe it is the circumstances,
which make it so tricky to finish our easy union
and knowing this will leave me wanting more
and more of you,
and both will be left over unpure,
without our ultimate completed satisfaction.

So as you start your day,
dashing hither and thither,
transporting your little people,
making your shopping errands,
answering the phone to friends and clients,
talking and discussing,
resolving deep emotional and troublesome negative disturbing issues.


And while watching the clock at five to three,
pushing away the tender moments,
we have shared will you know I have not stopped loving you
nor can I contemplate a deep breath,
without precious memories,
of you dancing around naked in my mind,
leaving my heart beating,
pounding strong feelings for you,
and perhaps,
maybe you can,
while sitting peacefully in your warm sanctuary
or is that tropical conservatory,
indulge me with a smile and languished,
languid fantasy,
with as always the slim possibility we can talk
and walk hand-in-hand to a new a different unpromised land.
