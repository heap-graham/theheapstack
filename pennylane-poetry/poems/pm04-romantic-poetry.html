"Romantic Poetry"

Roses are Red. Violets are Blue,
All I want to say is: “I Love You”

Please do be kind, when you read my rhyme,
As these are only words, which left unspoken -
Would leave my heart a little broken.

Fare ye well oh pretty Lady,
and realise this - What’s said is just a little token
(for you),
So you need not laugh
or cry
nor even shape a reply in any form my dear,

Just read and feel or perhaps just say that there’s no doubt,

This man is in Love with his rhyme and there’s clearly no other reason. (Duh!)
