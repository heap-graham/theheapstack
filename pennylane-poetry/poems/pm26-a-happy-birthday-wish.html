“A Happy Birthday Wish”


So a whole anniversary year has past
and I hope and pray that my ideal of a sweet, pretty, intelligent lady is happy,
on her birthday preparations and merrymaking,

And that as the new year of your life unfolds may all your wishes,
dreams come true,
and with that your growth in mind, body and soul will be faithful,
to your caring, kind, hearts beating desire,
and all you can manifest from your vivid, active, imagination,

It is a simple birthday pleasure to receive gifts and love from friends and family,
to know your appreciated and your life’s work,
effort and joy are blooming for you now,
perhaps if I had a magic wand and could grant any wish,
I wonder what that would be,
could it be a romantic dinner in Paris,
or a shopping spree in New York
or perchance that smooth, cool, sleazy, bluesy, jazz festival in Prague?

Or could it just be breakfast in bed,
with warmed slippers,
and a lazy day spent doing nothing,
but what pleases you most,
maybe a stroll down memory lane,
while reading a good book with your feet up?

After being loving hand washed by your fantasy lover,
and now you are bathed in warm white light in your sun drenched conservatory,
slipping into romantic day dreams of what is and what is not meant to be;
or for reasons of commitment and security
or a tall, mountain wall of cold fear can never be.

And then drifting away into a mystical journey into time and space,
flying around the cosmos,
seeking a universe of wonders,
a new born star
or even the glory of an infinite painted supernova,
or perhaps the birth of a new sparkling soul
or even to fly along with your golden, winged guardian angel on a splendid,
marvelous trip through your divine heaven,
moved loving by light fingertip caresses and gentle, soft kisses.

Or possibly a walk in the new green woods by a warm, clear, blue, still, glassy lake,
with sun shining, smiling on your every gentle footsteps into a glade of sweet scented succulent green grass,
surrounded by wild, rainbow coloured flowers and translucent,
graceful butterflies darting and flirting with the petals of nectar,

And cuddly woodland brown furry animals scampering and scurrying around,
on their busy round of natures daily chores,
with baby lambs suckling and scampering to be with the safety and warmth of their mums earthy underneath,
and birds and bees buzzing with life and energy,
feasting on the world’s many simple treasures.

Or even a picnic by the beach with a bursting hamper packed with glorious,
tasty foods, and cool, refreshing drinks,
and with friends, family and children singing nursery rhymes while building castles in the sands,
as you take a skinny dip in the cool, clear blue ocean, racing a swim,
with playful, gleaming, slippery, silvery dolphins who sing to you with a squeak
and a click a happy birthday song,
and with purposes do you feel God’s hands of joy applauding her perfect creation.


While all of this is naught but words unspoken;
loving verse to please and muse my ideal hypnotic lady,
what else can I say to make this a perfect day by wishing all my unconditional love for you,
with a sweet affectionate embrace
and with a rhyming dedication do I appreciate you on your birthday celebration,
with passionate, heartfelt, romantic, warm, velvet, wet kisses and wishing you,
for all life Gods gift of true bliss.
