“A Rational Analysis Of Love?”


While talking through all the in’s and out’s,
the to and the fro,
the push and the pull of our romantic and partial affair,
what I realise with my loving real eyes is you stand tall, distant
and remote beyond my gaze and phase our love with a rational distaste.

How easy for you to watch from left field
and narrate on my performance as spectator
or is that professional commentator?

While in play or is that now in replay?

As if you are only an innocent bystander,
coldly studying with indifference
and all you can do is whinge with dislike on my irrational, deranged, intricate
and intimate foreplay for you,

And with most prolonged analysis which is overdone and overcooked,
comes a paralysis of the mind,
strangely after all these months that I am now only a distant patient
and not perhaps a temperate hot flustered wet climate for
or of your affections.

As for my recollection in session and under your hypnotic suggestion,
you, unlike most in your vocation,
keep it simple,
look for the obvious,
as plain as the nose on your beautiful, caring, angels face
and then were kind to me with your healing words.

Keep it simple,
a repeated rhyming phrase,
is not what you now apply to our evident,
instinctive bond,
built on our effortless rapport;
an emotional understanding,
and challenging minds;
full of sparring ideas,
thoughtful conceptions,
and tangential interconnections,
toasted with warm loving reflections;
and it is this which is the basis of all that’s followed on our imperative wits,
which succeeds only now in an ultimate union;
stilted and still beyond our barring, difficult, uncompromising circumstances.

I am clear about my feelings for you with the strength, intensity of my natural being;
this does not placate our breakfast of passion where I as the bacon,
from a fallen, broken swine am I,
or should be, committed;
as insane for you,
do I now cry a river of lost tears,
and the fried, runny egg, with slippery yellow yolk deposit from you,
not as a mother hen,
only ever so slightly involved,
remote, yet you left seemingly incomplete,
leaving us both still wanting more;
whatever we can chance, even a slow, slow dance,
it is a puppets wish to be strung along hither and thither,
with his mistress wishes
and a marionette’s tears are orchestrated,
not with aplomb,
which only fall on dusty wood chippings,
falling wastefully on a space full of fears,
pulling hard on my heart strings.

What can I say to you to make it clear,
no more can you fumble around my past and explain a warm easy peaceful happy happening,
with cold hearted scrutiny which keeps your passions in check
and you away, at bay, with my curling, churlish dismay,

Let us move on past all of this,
will be the ringing voice within your mental aerobics of refusal
and for me very little compassion,
perhaps I am lazy and crazy for all of this
and have missed the point of breathing
and being still with my lot
and all of the blessing which surround me,
but to be honest I really do not think so,
so let us let it be,
agree to disagree,
but you have to come hard for me;
with your true needs and wants.


What is an easy truth and there is no falsehood in my moments,
which all it takes,
when I think of you,
yes of course I am blue,
but it is a simple response,
biological or maybe psychological,
and who cares whatever Freud might say,
is that if ever or when I am deep inside of you,
I know I will be at peace and will know a bliss,
that only God can give when I have passed away from this mortal coil,
with that you can know my divine easy truth;
which is of course that I love you with no conditions or reservations.
