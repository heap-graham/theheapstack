“Slowly letting go with no more dreams of you”


So one last rhyme on our passionate crime,
to be sure there is no doubt left in your mind on where I stand with my feelings and dreams of you,
as you wish I am letting go never to be in touch again,
with that you may think I am blue or still yearning for a good screw.

‘tis true that its hard to let go,
for these feelings which are about true and unconditional love,
yet to besmirch the happening would make my search for the one,
a lie never once to earn the respect of the fall of a lonely teardrop from your childlike face
or the tender touch from my fair hypnotic lady,
and to taste the sweet kisses of his blessed fallen angel,

You see the truth of this experience will not yield to emptiness
nor be broken by your abstinence
and if there is integrity in our connection,
then you like me will not be able to leave it be.

Do not mistake any arrogance in these words of love as there’s none here,
just the knowledge that the reality will grow
and endure and there is no escape from this for
either you or me,
an inevitable outcome of a devotion which is meant to be,

That is not to say you will sway and stray back to our private, secret meetings,
as this is absolute in its lack of honesty in the way we show the world of our adoration
and if we continue this we will just have a seedy proof of our deceit
and lack of compassion for our fellow man,
which has to stop or we declare our position with care and relish our plans.

No deep inside the feelings will mature and nothing we do will every stop this germ of hope and youth to flourish:
how or when I have no idea just that time will tell
and we have precious moments left for this truth to be revealed
and to dive deep into each others blue clear ocean,
suckling in our respective being like drunken men with an eternal thirst,

On reflection on actions I could take:
like to move with certainty to your door and force your hand by declaring how I feel
and bringing down everything I hold dear and true,
bringing grief and hurt for everyone and would break anything of beauty which we have held and treasured,
or maybe by careful deliberation find clever ways to weave into your life never letting you breathe in peace,
taunting you with pleasures and notions,
and the threat of specific exposure,
of your tranced occupation and your spiritual considerations,

No none of this would be the actions of a loving man and naught of these will I manifest by will
or wish, just do not think that I am not strong or that I am not ruthless, enough to do this,
and by not taking these actions you think and feel that I am sincere,
and not a coward full of fear and not prepared to live with the responsibility
and to face the consequences; bravely, intelligently have I applied myself for all my wishes to come true,

As its time to let it be, please do not think in days,
weeks and months to come that I will not remember
nor lose these feelings for you, as nothing could be further from my truth.


So leaving you to dwell on this caring verse,
will I meditate on a possibility of being with you,
so watch your dreams and your feelings too,
as while letting go in time and space will I continue to wish for a long loving life with you;
ideally brought about by love and care,
with cherished joyful moments and, of course,
praying for God’s kind loving bliss.
