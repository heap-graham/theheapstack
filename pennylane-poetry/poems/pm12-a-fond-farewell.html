“A fond farewell and heartfelt thank you to a sweet,
pretty and intelligent Lady”


This will be a simple rhyme which should not take much of your time,
for me to say that,
as you know already,
I love you
and that the moments we have shared will be my secret treasures to hide
and find while running around with you, naked, in my mind.

The beauty and the loveliness of you will wash across me like an oceans’ dew with waves of innocence
and desire will I breathe in your essence of a sweetly perfumed soul mate
and draw you deep into my lowest and highest feelings of loving you.

As I write these words I have a realisation that no more can I communicate too you in clever and tricky verse,
designed to talk to your inside mind
and hence calculated to beat a path to your heart by using romantic traps and lusty back door gaps;
and you know that’s not a very smart way to court,
and perhaps maybe not such a cunning plan for the sweet, pretty and intelligent (foxy) Lady,
who can spot the leopards of any witless man.

And while we are now apart I have eyes of tears in-waiting to roll down
and sting salt in my sad, confused face
and the weight of your loss,
as I know we will no longer meet,
is almost too much to bare
and portends to crack open my chest
and will,
if I am not careful,
shatter my heart to kingdom come.

Now my stomach hurts too where once there was a warm glow is now replaced by a dull and constant flood of numbing pain.

Our time together was so beautiful, lovely and gentle:
Oh Sweet Lady!

This time went so fast like a blink of an eye
and now you are going out of my life for good.

And your reasons are so correct and faithful to you
and what you hold dear,
your children and your hypnotic occupation,
and no one could hurt that bond of real love.

You have woken-up myself and given me the gift of knowing true and unconditional love
and from my deepest parts of myself I thank you and will hold you dear for all time.

This, as said, is a simple, short rhyme for in time and space I have touched your face,
called your name and felt some grace.

No longer do I need to capture your deepest being,
to have had a glimpse of real love,
can I say what a joy that is to let it be
and have these gentle feelings course through me.

I know you now
and can see your truth
and your decisions made are proof of that precious thing we call love.

I will, nor cannot help, my feelings for you which are deep within.
And no longer do I have pain or sorrow as you are not with me,
as a part of you will always walk with me
and I have this truth which is beyond the pale of a physical touch and outside the realms of normal space and time.

So this is a human goodbye,
which of course has no meaning in God’s good grace,
and when in mind I recall the looks of joy on your face,
while being kissed and enjoying bliss,
will I have cherished moments which I can ponder on,
and for which will fuel over an eternity,
a love which will last forever and ever,
thanks be to the One.
