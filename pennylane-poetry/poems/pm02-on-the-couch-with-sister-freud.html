On the Couch With Sister Freud

Love in this moment.

On rainy days,
when I am blue,
I call my Sister Freud for professional help,
As you see,
My Lady,
friend is what I think she is,
will listen and not blink or hang me to the kitchen sink.

We sit and talk of issues in my distant past,
my Mother!
My private losses and of grievances last,

And play we do with memories gone and even sing and dance a song,
Sometimes with a trance or two she even takes me to a place of Truth.

While realizing this special place is created in time
and space and I am no equal to this Angels grace,

So, Even though I’m on the clock,
and money changes hands in a way which means:
we cannot kiss,
hold hands or share our bliss.

And “Tick Tock - you’re off!” is her only chant.

I am not sure why it’s such a pleasure to be with one of such a measure ...
One who is calm and radiant,
and which makes me misty and slightly dizzy,
and in short it’s a kind of love which of course cannot be fizzy.

Dare I say that I cannot heal any more
without her trance to bring me to the fore?
It would be wrong for me to be a burden,
and yet I know now nothing could be,
and no more professional help will I see.


It’s raining now and it’s very blue,
did I say that: “I Love You”.
