“Our Six Rules of Engagement - working only by Arrangement?”


Perhaps it is simpler to call it a day,
and let us shake hands as departing,
soon to be long lost, friends,
and pretend I am moving to Southend,
on a high note and go on our separate ways,
heaven knows the irresistible emotional roller-coaster joy ride seems not to be worth the,
grief stricken,
inflated ego price: too many unknowns left to dice!

And you, as always, seem to know best
and like all the rest with jolly good advice:
no-one I know thinks this is smart idea;
as we are a bit mentally queer - like most folk - but we had to put it to the test
and see what is what between us,
like a little sexual excursion;
a real lively hot blooded fantasy tour:
to feel orgasmic fireworks alight,
once again,
or perchance even just to see if there was a real,
but slim,
possibility of you and me – do you not agree?

For me I am clueless,
I have no idea- for love?
Maybe?

And me thinks yes that is for the best,
I will be able to go to bed and have a good trouble free rest,
let it go,
let bygones be bygones now,
and then I try to let it lie,
and fail to fall asleep,
as I am crying my heart out,
and my soul burns away my third eye,
screaming at me for letting you go.

(Like I had a choice–not! You spigot! (That is a stopcock, for sure now. You silly bee! Bet you think that I am truly crazy.))

And I,
my mind or ego state retaliates,
with a simple rhetorical question to the
quiet,
distant,
cold,
remote universe:
“For God’s sake I wonder why, why? Let us let sleeping dogs lie!”

And then I remember the slow start of our emotional,
gentle,
and sensual connection,
an easy bliss just to sit and talk with no pressure to gawk at you,
and from a simple declaration of subtle feelings,
I tumbled head-over-heals in love with you,
and wished to experience you complete,
you know,
like in mind, body and soul,
and realise it would be so replete,
and to-date proved our love has potential
and potency, as we connect across our mental and physical chess board:
with no check mate insight,
more like a dull stale mated end!

And my idol has taken and thrown away all the pieces!
So are there any moves left?
Perhaps a queen sacrifice or an ‘En Passant’,
always a surprise pawn move,
can we ask Sherlock Homes what to do?

Wonder what he would say:
“Remove the impossible and what is left, however improbable, is the solution,
which is,
of course,
elementary my dear (dry) Watson.”
(A rose by any other name!)

And what can we rule in and out?
Lets not leave our partners,
a vital key for one,
keep our relationship private for two (That’s sacrosanct!),
we communicate within office hours for three,
that leaves us with our stolen meetings
and by making each one - the Last!
do we with hand-on-heart never have a continuation of on-going relations for four,
and with no expectations or terms
or conditions to keep our bee hive alive for five,
and the solid golden rule of our engagement is lets keep conception out of sex for six;
just be careful, mindful, we can role play instead of going all the way – ok ?

Thinking out-of-the-box
and this is far from a good detox,
can we not find a creative endeavor which to work on:
like discussing poetry
or writing a good romantic book
or build on a common interest;
like say, day dreaming away afternoons in a sauna?


So to close with a long goodbye,
I look east and west and find no rational compass to give me direction,
with our hidden and intimate connection,
bounded are we by rules of social and moral etiquette
and it is not a question of right or wrong,
for me,
as my dedication of you is borne from adoring feelings.
made in my pure open heart,
riveted in true love;
an incurable condition,
which can be healed by your caring hands and sweet kisses.

So you have my needed love potion – will you dispense for or of me?
