“When Old and Grey will I love ye?”


I begin to wonder, without any thunder and as always I am trying not to blunder,
what will become of our love as we grow old and pale,
and we have not moved into a fateful slumber.

As you see I feel this love is meant to be,
and with careful management, maybe,
could last a long, long time, as has most would,
I crave a moment, in the long distant future,
on a park, sun drenched, bench,
with you and a cup of sweet tea,
reflecting on our life’s joy and misery,
while holding hands and stirring each others hearts with loving words
and fond, simple loving gestures, slightly topped with light finger tipped caresses,

With you as my long standing loving wench,
while old and grey would I penetrate your mind with feelings
and actions which have not gone astray or put aside on a dirty ashtray.
Would all of this disappear to a point of infinity,
no longer a concern of you and me,
as the bustling crowd will let us be,
and of course our hearts could beat true!

No more will you pine for faith and truth as your children have flown from the coop,

And with no spouse to pester your loving siesta will you be devoted to the one,
who truly loves you, well perhaps,
maybe I can only dream!
And possibly we have had no time to consummate our passionate crime,
and as old and aged soul mates we can manage to hold hands,
remember our stolen bliss and forever have sweet kisses,

And while I recant all the rhyming words unspoken
and can remember the first feelings of our eternal youth,
Will we then consolidate all these mimes and dimes which have no longer a sentence of silence
or secrecy or the harsh judgement of a silly society?

And our passion can unleash in simple treasured caress
and loving ample kisses,
As a Lady old would I have the faith and charity to behold your beauty with a simple, warm hold,
with a little naughty word and phrase which would catapult you to the bloom of your youth.

Perhaps this is again idle speculation of a man,
who passion spurts hot with a passionate flood,
remembering his greatest joys of manhood,
whose seventh heaven is yours to grant!
And hopefully their be no more rants and scants with hopeless recants!

And maybe ‘tis a man who knows his soul
and knows deep within that it is his greatest joy is to join with a true soul mate,
just like you, ye cannot think I have no ken of this!

And with God’s blessing we will be able to create with heavenly inspiration our own final destination.
All of this has to be done in the knowing of our true free will and that
as separate souls, God’s greatest gift could be higher than our simple bliss and union,


And as I contemplate all these spiritual dimensions, I ponder whether I can keep you
from a true, true light, that my simple candle light will be enough to burn and appreciate
your holy worth
and by unselfish deeds will I not keep you from the One true bliss.
