"A Chant for a Soul Mate."


So I begin with the feelings within which started in all innocence from sitting
and talking with you,
and the happiness that grows from no demands
or needs except to be near to you,

And now I try and share a chant for you with the depth of that glow
and that I am true to my hearts desire
and truest thoughts of you.

What’s more I have to say is that all of me has started too grow
and the puzzle of myself is beginning to unravel,
for the best,
breathe in and watch out
and I guarantee you’ll see something special!

Maybe ‘tis true that all this is about a bodily screw.

Then there are ideas and notions within my heart which point a way forward,
which goes beyond the root chakra
and paints shooting stars across the sky’s of a richly devout paradise,

You see I suspect and I think you’ll agree that in this life,
we are composed of three parts which make us whole: body, mind and soul
and our hearts the communication tool of our deepest communion,

with the only One Soul.

And while in meditation on working with my chakras again,
it seems that just the thought of you will leave a warm glow in my sacral one,
you know in my tum,
and as we stand head-to-toe that you will open all of the vibrant ones,
from tum to throat including the power one at the sternum,
and my passionate heart,
this is all lined up like a ribbon of energy and colour,
and for what its worth ‘tis a lot more fun than shifting the energy centers,
while sitting at home on your own!

And when alone searching for sleep and rest
and I ponder on your cherished mystery,
my third eye starts to wake
and shake my brain,
from the dizzy circles of the mind which enslaves all of my moments,
and lets me know that the connection with you,
is beyond the norm of space and time.

So my mantra now its parts unfold first with a knowledge to be
and breathe in the moment,
then to learn to love
and heal myself and thus leaving me to love
and heal others too,
including you,
and from this chant I also know that acceptance of all of myself
and others is a vital key along with two others.

That the power of change is my own responsibility
and with focus and good intent I can create a tiny miracle here and there,
and more’s to come,
if I say out loud to the cosmos whatever other miracles are created for you
and me I am pleased for them to be.

And finally my mantra is almost complete,
with only one part left to be said and done
and it’s simply this,
to give thanks to the One
and to know that life is sacred
and the real part of heaven.

And there’s just one other verse I like to say to you,
as another loving soul mate,
is a big thank-you,
of whom I would like for you to be is my yin and my yang
and that I can idle all of my time to kiss
and share in all of our bliss!
