Welcome


Welcome to Pennylane Poetry and Short Adult Stories.


Pennylane Poetry is a celebration of love, unrequited love and human connection. Through poetry and the spoken word, potential unresolved feelings between two people are given a voice.

Pennylane Poetry is a collection of romantic poetry and short adult stories celebrating love, desire, intimacy, humour and the many emotions that may connect people in life.

Each poem and story reflects a moment, a memory, a dream, an experience, or an unfulfilled wish.

Pennylane, our loved Border Collie, became the inspiration for the name of this collection.

Thank you for visiting and I hope you enjoy your stay.
